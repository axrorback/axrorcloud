$ axrorcloud — The HTTP Tunneling Tool

axrorcloud is a powerful CLI tool that allows you to expose your local server to the internet easily. With axrorcloud, you can access your local services from anywhere, without complex configurations or setups.

# Usage

First, obtain your personal auth token by logging in at the site:

axrorcloud --auth <AUTH_TOKEN>

Expose your local service to the internet:

axrorcloud --port <YOUR_PORT>

You can also specify a custom subdomain:

axrorcloud --port <YOUR_PORT> --sd <YOUR_SUBDOMAIN>
You can stop the tunnel by pressing Ctrl + C in the terminal.
